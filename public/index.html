About HIFAZAT
Your Personal Emergency Response System
What does this app do?
HIFAZAT keeps you safe in emergencies. With a single tap of the SOS button, your trusted emergency contacts are instantly alerted with your exact location — so help can reach you fast, when every second counts.
How it works
📝
1. Create Your Account
Sign up with your name, city and phone. Your password is securely encrypted.
👥
2. Add Emergency Contacts
Save up to 2 trusted responders — family or friends.
🆘
3. Press SOS When in Danger
One tap dispatches an instant alert with your live GPS location.
📲
4. Contacts Get Notified
SMS + WhatsApp delivery with a Google Maps link to find you.
